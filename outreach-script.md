# Better World Agent — Partner Outreach Script
*For reaching out to peak bodies, networks, and digital inclusion orgs*

---

## Before you send anything

You're not pitching your services. You're pitching a partnership.
Their members get a genuinely useful free session. You get warm access to your target clients.

Make it easy to say yes. Ask for one small thing.

---

## Email — Cold outreach to partner orgs

**Subject:** AI session for your members (practical, no fluff)

---

Hi [Name],

I work with not-for-profits on AI adoption — specifically helping staff use AI tools to save time and do more of the work that matters, without replacing anyone.

I'm reaching out to [Organisation Name] because your members are exactly the kind of mission-driven orgs who could benefit, and who often don't have a clear entry point into AI.

I'd love to offer a free 60–90 minute session for a small group of your members — practical, hands-on, no jargon. The kind of thing where people leave with tools they can use the next day.

If it's useful, we can look at rolling it out wider or building something more tailored.

Worth a quick chat?

Matt
Better World Agent
betterworldagent.com

---

## What to personalise (don't skip this)

- **[Name]** — Find the actual program manager or partnerships contact. Not "To Whom It May Concern."
- **One specific reference** to their work: "I saw you're running X program" or "Your recent report on Y resonated with me." One line. Shows you did 10 minutes of homework.
- **Keep it short.** The script above is the maximum length, not the minimum.

---

## Follow-up (send if no reply after 5–7 days)

**Subject:** Re: AI session for your members

Hi [Name],

Just following up on the below — happy to keep it simple.

If there's a team call, member event, or webinar coming up where a 30-minute AI intro would be useful, I'm available to jump in.

No obligation to make it anything more than that.

Matt

---

## If they respond positively — what to say next

Don't over-complicate it. Agree on a date, format, and audience size.

Then send a 3-line summary of what the session covers:

> "The session covers: (1) where AI actually saves NFPs time right now, (2) hands-on with 2–3 tools relevant to their work, (3) how to introduce AI to staff in a way that builds trust, not anxiety. About 60–90 mins. Works best with 5–20 people."

---

## If they ask about cost

"The pilot session is free — I'm building my case study base and want to make sure the content is genuinely useful before I commercialise it. If it lands well, we can talk about a paid rollout."

*(This is honest, positions you well, and removes all friction for the yes.)*

---

## If they ask who else you've worked with

You're pre-revenue. Don't bluff. Say this:

"I'm currently in the pilot phase — this is my first round of sessions with partner organisations. My background is in systems training and AI adoption in [government contracting context], and I'm bringing that into the NFP space. Happy to share more about my approach if that's helpful."

Confident, honest, no apology.
