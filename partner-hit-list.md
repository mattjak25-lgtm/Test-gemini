# Better World Agent — National Partner Hit List
*Last updated: April 2026 | Pre-revenue phase*

---

## How to use this list

Don't pitch your services. Pitch a **partnership**.
Your ask is always one of:
- A pilot webinar/session for their members
- An intro to 2–3 NFPs in their network
- Co-delivery of an AI capability session

One yes here = warm access to dozens of NFPs.

---

## Tier 1 — Highest Priority (approach first)

### 1. Good Things Foundation Australia
**Why:** National reach, explicitly funds digital skills training, already running train-the-trainer programs. Grant angle is built in — they fund the work that funds you.
**What they need:** Practical AI capability for community workers and digitally excluded populations.
**Your pitch:** "AI tools for community workers — save 5 hours/week, no tech background needed."
**Fast entry:** Contact their program team. Offer a free pilot session for 1–2 of their partner orgs.
**Contact:** goodthings.org.au/australia → Programs team
**Grant connection:** They fund digital mentor programs. Your engagement can be grant-funded through them.

---

### 2. Infoxchange
**Why:** National. Already sells tech + training into NFPs. Massive existing client base. Weak on practical AI training right now — that's your opening.
**What they need:** AI capability content they can offer their existing clients without building it themselves.
**Your pitch:** "We plug in as your AI capability partner — you handle the relationship, we deliver the training."
**Fast entry:** Pitch a co-delivered webinar to their client base. Low risk for them, high exposure for you.
**Contact:** infoxchange.org → Partnerships

---

### 3. ACOSS (Australian Council of Social Service)
**Why:** Peak body for the community services sector nationally. Their members ARE your target clients.
**What they need:** Practical resources helping member organisations operate more efficiently.
**Your pitch:** "AI productivity training for NFP leaders — responsible adoption, staff empowerment focus."
**Fast entry:** Conference breakout session or member webinar.
**Contact:** acoss.org.au → Events/Programs team

---

### 4. Pro Bono Australia
**Why:** They're the media/community hub for the NFP sector. An article or webinar with them puts you in front of tens of thousands of NFP leaders overnight.
**What they need:** Practical, values-aligned content their audience will engage with.
**Your pitch:** "Guest article: How NFPs can use AI to 10X their impact without replacing staff" + webinar offer.
**Fast entry:** Pitch an article first. Relationship builds from there.
**Contact:** probonoaustralia.com.au → Editorial team

---

## Tier 2 — Strong fit, slightly slower burn

### 5. Volunteering Australia
**Why:** National network. Member orgs have zero AI capability and enormous admin burden — perfect fit for your offer.
**Your pitch:** "AI tools for volunteer coordination and admin reduction."
**Fast entry:** Member webinar or conference session.

---

### 6. Community Council for Australia
**Why:** Peak body representing the broader NFP/charity sector. Policy-focused but strong member events.
**Your pitch:** AI governance and productivity for NFP leadership teams.
**Fast entry:** Contribute to their member comms or events program.

---

### 7. Social Ventures Australia (SVA)
**Why:** Works with mission-driven orgs on capacity building. Your values-based framing fits their language perfectly.
**Your pitch:** "AI as a capacity-building lever — not cost-cutting, impact-scaling."
**Fast entry:** Webinar for their portfolio orgs.

---

### 8. ASBAS Digital Solutions (government-funded, national)
**Why:** Government program that pays providers to deliver low-cost digital training to small businesses. You register as a provider, they send you clients.
**Your pitch:** Register as a delivery partner, not a grant applicant.
**Fast entry:** Apply as a registered adviser/provider.
**Note:** This skips the grant waiting game entirely.

---

## Tier 3 — Worth pursuing once you have 1–2 wins

### 9. Impact Boom (social enterprise community)
**Why:** Strong national community of social entrepreneurs and mission-driven founders. Podcast, events, directory.
**Fast entry:** Podcast guest pitch. One episode = national NFP/startup audience.

---

### 10. The Difference Incubator (TDi)
**Why:** Social enterprise accelerator with national reach. Their cohort orgs are exactly your target client.
**Fast entry:** Offer a session for current cohort. Build relationship with program team.

---

### 11. Philanthropy Australia
**Why:** Represents foundations and grant-makers. Understanding this network helps you help your clients find funding.
**Fast entry:** Member events. Position yourself as the "AI for grantees" expert.
