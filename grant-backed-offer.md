## The part most consultants don't tell you

**Many NFPs can fund this through existing grants.**

You may already be eligible for digital capability funding through programs like:

- **Good Things Foundation Australia** — up to $52,500 for digital skills and capability uplift
- **Telstra Connected Communities** — up to $10,000 for technology training and digital inclusion
- **auDA Community Grant Program** — digital innovation and internet capability projects
- **Local council and foundation grants** — $2,000–$40,000 for workforce capability and digital transformation

The key: these grants fund *"digital capability uplift"* and *"workforce productivity transformation"* — which is exactly what this program delivers. You don't need a line item called "AI training."

**We help you identify the right grant and position the application.** In many cases, the program pays for itself.
